Brand Overview
What Madar Randa Is

Madar Randa is a Saudi engineering institution with over 30 years of uninterrupted operation in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The company specialises in the supply, installation, and long-term maintenance of automatic doors, automatic windows, sectional and industrial doors, loading dock equipment, road barriers, parking control systems, security entrances, and smart access solutions.

What makes Madar Randa distinctive is not the range of its products — it is the depth of its engineering knowledge and the continuity of its client relationships. Three decades of operation means Madar Randa has served the same clients across generations of infrastructure projects, from the original installation through to ongoing maintenance and system upgrades.

The company serves every vertical where engineered access matters: residential villas, commercial towers, hospitals, hotels, airports, government facilities, warehouses, and factories. Its client base ranges from individual homeowners to architects, developers, and government procurement offices.

Market Analysis — Saudi Arabia
Market Context

Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 infrastructure programme is the primary demand driver for Madar Randa's services. Giga-projects including NEOM, Red Sea Project, Diriyah, Qiddiya, and Expo Riyadh are creating unprecedented demand for premium access control and automatic door systems across commercial, hospitality, aviation, and residential verticals.

The Saudi market for automatic doors and access control systems was valued at approximately SAR 2.8 billion in 2024, with a CAGR of 9–12% driven by smart building integration, security mandates, and Vision 2030 construction. The premium segment — where reliability, safety certification, and long-term maintenance matter — is underpenetrated and dominated by a handful of established operators.

Color Psychology — Why This Palette

Deep Navy (#0C1E3C): Navy communicates institutional trust, precision, and authority. It is the colour of engineering firms, aerospace companies, and premium financial institutions. For a Saudi engineering company serving government and aviation clients, navy signals that you are a serious organisation. It triggers associations with reliability and technical competence — exactly what an automatic door company needs to project.

Accent Blue (#2B6CB0): The accent blue introduces energy and modernity without abandoning the trust palette. It differentiates CTAs and interactive elements, creating visual hierarchy that guides the user toward conversion actions. Blue is the colour most strongly associated with trust across cultures — a critical factor for B2B decision-makers.

Engineering Silver (#A8B4C8): Silver is the colour of precision machinery, metal engineering, and high-grade components. It connects the brand visually to the products — steel doors, aluminium frames, precision motors. It is never decorative; it is always structural.

Rules
Brand Guidelines
Logo Usage Rules
✓ Correct Logo Usage
Use on navy, white, or silver-pale backgrounds only. Always maintain safe area of 2× letterform height. Use full wordmark for all print applications over 80mm. Use icon mark for digital favicons only.
✗ Never Do This
Never stretch, rotate, or recolour the logo. Never place on busy photographic backgrounds without an overlay. Never use outdated logo versions. Never reduce below 32px height digital / 20mm print.
Color Usage Rules
✓ Approved Color Combinations
Navy background + White text. White background + Navy text. Navy background + Accent Blue CTAs. Silver Pale background + Navy or Charcoal text. All combinations must meet WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1).
✗ Forbidden Color Uses
No bright red, orange, or green anywhere in the brand. No gradients on text. No purple. No neon or fluorescent colors. No competing accent colors alongside the defined Accent Blue.
Typography Rules
Display Type Rules
Barlow Condensed only for H1, H2, headlines, section titles, and all-caps labels. Minimum 700 weight for headlines. Maximum letter-spacing: –0.03em on large display. Never use italic on condensed display.
Body Type Rules
Barlow Regular (400) for all body text. Barlow Medium (500) for emphasis within body. Minimum body size: 14px digital, 10pt print. Line height: 1.65–1.8 for body, 1.1–1.2 for display.
Accessibility Rules
Color Contrast
All text must pass WCAG 2.1 AA. Large text (18px+ or bold 14px+): minimum 3:1. Normal text: minimum 4.5:1. Navy on White: 16.3:1 ✓. Accent Blue on White: 4.6:1 ✓.
Digital Accessibility
All interactive elements must have visible focus states (3px navy outline). All images must have descriptive alt text in Arabic and English. Form labels must be visible (not placeholder-only). Font minimum 16px for body on mobile.
Brand Consistency Rules — The Non-Negotiables
  • The company name is always written "Madar Randa" in English (not MADAR RANDA or Madar-Randa)
  • The Arabic name مدار رندا always appears alongside the English name on bilingual materials
  • The tagline "Engineering Access Solutions" is always in Barlow Condensed, sentence case
  • Photography must always show engineering quality — no stock photography with generic doors
  • Never use the brand in a way that suggests cheap pricing or budget positioning
  • All website headlines must follow the Voice & Tone guide — no exclamation marks
  • Statistical claims ("30+ years", "500+ projects") must always be defensible and accurate
  • The accent blue is reserved for CTAs and active UI elements — not for decorative use
  • Icons must always be the same outlined engineering/technical style — no decorative illustration
  • The brand must always read as premium in its own context — not relative to a generic corporate standard

The deep navy, condensed typography, and engineering-precision aesthetic are durable enough to carry the brand for decades — they should be refined, not reinvented.
